# project's name (50 characters)
IR power switch for NucBox G5

# summarize your project (140 characters)
Add IR power switch to a mini PC to turn on it with a TV remote.

# describe your project (1000 characters)
I am going to use this tiny PC to watch netflix on my living room.
So it would be handy if I am able to power on the PC with a TV remote.
This project aims to achieve it without compromising compactness of the PC.

# Details
<p>In mini PCs, power switch is soldered to a board directly and I need custom solution.</p>

<p>What I need to do is simple:</p>

<ol>
  <li>Find out which pin is the pin to turn on and attach a cable to it to connect to a mcu.</li>
  <li>Find out what can be used to power mcu.</li>
  <li>Connect IR receiver to mcu.</li>
  <li>Program mcu to output low to power switch pin when it received particular remote control signal.</li>
</ol>
